Chunking, embeddings, and what overlap costs

Three levers decide whether a video RAG index can find the right ten seconds: how wide a chunk is, how much of it the next chunk repeats, and which weights encode it. Every number on this page was measured on evals/dev by make sweep — move a lever and read what happened, rather than what should have.

Recall came back low. Check these, in this order.

Every one of these has produced a wrong number in this repo at least once, and the chunker was the first suspect every time. It was the chunker once.

  1. Is the embedder the quantisation you think it is? ollama pull hf.co/<repo> with no tag takes the repo's smallest file. Here that is Q2_K, and it halved recall@5 relative to :F16 — a bigger swing than every chunking lever on this page combined. 
    ollama list        # the tag is in the name; :latest is not a version, it is the smallest file
  2. Is the index one generation of chunks, or two? Chunk ids are <video_id>_<t_start>_<t_end>, so moving a lever changes the ids and the old rows stay behind. An index holding both scores better than either, for no reason the levers explain. 
    make index-dev INDEX_FLAGS=--reset    # after any chunk lever moves
  3. What else is in the index? recall@k is a property of the whole store, not of the chunker. Two videos that are not in the corpus were added to this repo's ./chroma at some point, and the Phase 1 gate fell from 0.9167 to 0.8333 with no lever touched — 521 extra chunks competing for five slots, and the most fragile pair losing its place. Filtered back to the four corpus videos it is 0.9167 again. 
    make sources       # every video_id the store actually holds
  4. Is dev ∩ heldout still empty? No gate result counts until this holds — a number tuned against a question that is also in the sealed set is not a measurement of anything. 
    make leakage-check
  5. Is the miss a wrong video, or the right video at the wrong second? Different bugs. Wrong video is a retrieval problem; right video, wrong second, is almost always a chunk wider than the ±30 s citation tolerance. The gate prints which, per pair. 
    make gate-phase1
  6. Only then, move a window. One lever, on evals/dev, three attempts, then escalate. Never on evals/heldout. 
    make sweep-dry     # what a lever does to the index, in seconds
make sweep         # what it does to recall, in ~20 minutes
